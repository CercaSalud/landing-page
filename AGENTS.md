# CercaSalud Landing Project Guidance

## Product scope

CercaSalud is validating a service concept for families supporting older adults. A wearable may allow the wearer to request help or flag a possible anomaly. The proposed next steps are human verification and, where available and appropriate, coordination of occasional in-person assistance.

This project is Phase 0: a promotional page for explaining the idea and validating interest. Do not present the service as live unless the project owner confirms it. Never imply guaranteed dispatch, response times, diagnosis, continuous nursing, hospital replacement, or emergency-service replacement.

## Audience and voice

- Write public copy in clear, natural Venezuelan Spanish.
- Address the adult child or trusted relative while treating the older adult with dignity and agency.
- Be calm, empathetic, and practical. Avoid fear, guilt, clinical jargon, and claims that families need constant surveillance.
- Explain that automated alerts are prompts for human verification, not diagnoses.

## Data and trust

- Keep interest-form collection disabled until an approved privacy notice and deletion-request channel are published.
- Collect only contact details and general area for validation. Never request health histories, diagnoses, medications, or identity numbers.
- Make clear the page and form are not emergency channels.
- Do not invent service areas, responder qualifications, partner relationships, operating procedures, or testimonials.

## Technical direction

Keep the site a lightweight static Astro page. Preserve accessibility, responsive layouts, low client-side JavaScript, and the project's no-collection-by-default behavior.
